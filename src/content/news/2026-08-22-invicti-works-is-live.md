Invicti.Works is open. We build mobile applications and the web platforms behind
them, for organizations that need software their people will actually use.

We started this because of a pattern we kept seeing from opposite directions —
one of us inside mission-driven organizations making do with tools that did not
fit, the other bringing new technology into large institutions that struggled to
absorb it. In both cases the software was rarely the hard part. Scoping it
honestly, shipping something small enough to finish, and staying with it after
launch — that was the hard part.

So that is how we work. More on what we build over on
[what we do](/products), and if you have something in mind,
[tell us about it](/contact).
