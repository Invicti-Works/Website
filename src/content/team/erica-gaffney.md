Erica co-founded Invicti.Works. Alongside it she is Executive Managing Director
of RTS Consulting Group, which she has led since 2023, and Director of
Development and Communications at Specially Adapted Resource Centers (SPARC), a
Northern Virginia nonprofit running community-based day programs for adults with
multiple and severe disabilities.

RTS Consulting Group is where the technical and commercial sides of her work
meet. It handles nonprofit and small business development end to end —
fundraising innovation, IT management, strategic planning and leadership
development — which means she spends as much time on the systems an organization
depends on as on the strategy behind them.

She has been building and funding programs for a decade. Before SPARC she was
Director of Development at OAR NOVA, working with people involved in the
criminal justice system across Fairfax, Loudoun and Prince William counties. She
co-founded Kids For Causes NOVA in 2018, connecting families with local
volunteer opportunities. Earlier, on ServiceSource's Warrior Bridge program, she
ran employment and training case management for veterans with disabilities —
recruitment and assessment through to job development and follow-up.

Erica holds a master's in Forensic Psychology from The George Washington
University and a master's in Criminal Justice from the University at Albany. She
teaches anger management, employability and life skills at the Loudoun County
Adult Detention Center, and has led a Girl Scout troop for thirteen years.
