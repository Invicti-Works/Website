Erica is a development and operations executive with nearly two decades spent
turning mission into funded, measurable work: writing the proposals, building
the donor programs, running the campaigns, and holding the reporting that keeps
funders coming back. She currently leads grant and contract management for a
nonprofit serving adults with disabilities, where she builds and manages
multimillion-dollar funding portfolios across federal, state, and private
sources. At a Northern Virginia reentry organization before that, she grew
funding 127% in two years and never missed a submission deadline, which reads
like a small statistic until you have watched one missed deadline take an
organization's entire year with it.

Erica came to fundraising from the other side of the service. She started her
career as a forensic case manager, advocating for clients in court and
coordinating their care across providers, then spent four years as a veteran
employment and training case manager helping people translate military service
into a civilian career. She holds a master's in forensic psychology from The
George Washington University and a master's in criminal justice from the State
University of New York at Albany. She knows exactly what a line on a grant
report buys, because she used to be the person delivering it.

That perspective is why she founded Real Transformative Solutions Consulting
Group, a practice built to give nonprofits the capacity they rarely have room to
build for themselves: grant strategy, board development, organizational
assessment, strategic planning, and mediation among stakeholders who all want
the same outcome by different routes. Earlier she co-founded Kids for Causes
NOVA, a regional volunteer program connecting families with local nonprofits,
and designed the training that made young volunteers genuinely useful rather
than merely present. Across every role the through line holds: find the
resources, build the plan, and hand the organization something it can run
without her.
