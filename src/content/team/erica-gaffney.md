Erica is also Executive Managing Director of RTS Consulting Group and Director
of Development and Communications at SPARC, a Northern Virginia nonprofit
running day programs for adults with severe and multiple disabilities.

She has spent a decade building and funding programs, and as much time on the
systems an organization depends on as on the strategy behind them. She holds
master's degrees in Forensic Psychology and Criminal Justice.
