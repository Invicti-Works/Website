Josh co-founded Invicti.Works. He is Director, Defense at Esri, the geospatial
software company, and sits on the advisory council at Xelevate as a multi-domain
operations subject-matter expert — Xelevate runs a 66-acre unmanned systems
testing and development facility inside an hour of Washington, D.C., with FAA
compliant airspace.

He has spent a decade taking emerging technology into national security
customers, usually early. At Ayasdi he ran national security and government
programs for explainable AI. At Thomson Reuters Special Services he built big
data and analytics into delivered solutions for government. At Slingshot
Aerospace — venture-backed, tracking space objects through a global sensor
network and applying AI across the tasking, collection, processing, exploitation
and dissemination lifecycle — he arrived as Vice President of Business
Development for the Department of Defense and moved into the Chief of Operations
seat.

The commercial side runs alongside it. He was Senior Vice President at Core4ce,
and Division Vice President for Strategy and Growth and Deputy General Manager
at Jacobs. At The Buffalo Group, across two stints, he was Senior Director of
Strategy, Director of Capture and Business Development, Director of Intelligence
Services and Senior IDIQ Program Manager — growth and delivery, not one or the
other.

He started as an Intelligence Specialist in the US Navy, then spent two years as
a SOF operations integrator supporting special mission units worldwide. That
community is still where he gives his time: he serves on the board of the
Donovan and Bank Foundation, helping special operators transition to civilian
life, and spent eight years with the Spookstock Foundation — latterly on its
board of advisors and as Executive Director of Programs — which has raised over
$4.8 million and mentored more than 130 Gold Star children of fallen
intelligence and special operations personnel.
