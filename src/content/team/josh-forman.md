Josh is also Director, Defense at Esri, the geospatial software company, and
advises Xelevate on multi-domain operations.

He has spent a decade taking emerging technology into national security
customers — explainable AI at Ayasdi, big data and analytics at Thomson Reuters
Special Services, and space-domain awareness at Slingshot Aerospace, where he
ran business development for the Department of Defense before becoming Chief of
Operations. He started as an Intelligence Specialist in the US Navy.
