## The problem

A mobile app is the visible part of a system. Behind it sit accounts, data,
permissions, integrations with whatever an organization already runs, and the
admin screens staff use daily. That part is easy to underestimate and expensive
to get wrong — and when it is neglected, it is usually what limits the product
later.

## How we work on it

We build the platform as a product in its own right, not as scaffolding. That
means an API designed to be integrated against, permissions that still make
sense at ten times the users, and admin tooling your team does not dread
opening.

Sometimes there is no mobile app at all and the platform is the whole product.
That is fine — the approach does not change.

## Who it is for

Teams who need a web platform, an API, or the back end for a mobile product, and
want it built to be maintained rather than rewritten in two years.
