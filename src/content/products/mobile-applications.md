## The problem

Most mobile projects do not fail at the code. They fail at scope that keeps
growing, a release process nobody owns, or a first version so large it never
quite gets finished. By the time an app reaches the store, the reason for
building it has often moved on.

## How we work on it

We scope to a first release that is small enough to actually ship, then build
toward it. Design and engineering run together rather than in sequence, so
decisions get tested against what is buildable while changing them is still
cheap.

Store submission is part of the job, not an afterthought — review guidelines,
signing, metadata and screenshots included. After release we stay on for the
part that matters most: watching how the app gets used and shipping the changes
that follow from it.

## Who it is for

Organizations that need a mobile product and want one team accountable for it
end to end, rather than coordinating a designer, a developer and an agency
themselves.
