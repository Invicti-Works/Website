## What we do

Invicti.Works is a small software company. We design and build mobile
applications for iOS and Android, along with the web platforms, services and
integrations that sit behind them.

Most of what we take on starts before there is anything to build — an idea, a
problem someone keeps running into, a manual process that has outgrown its
spreadsheet. We do the work of turning that into something with a defined shape:
what the first version does, who it is for, and what can wait. Then we build it,
put it in front of real users, and keep going.

We are deliberately small. That means we are selective about what we take on,
and it means the people who scope your project are the people who build it.

## How we work

**Start with the problem.** Before anything gets designed, we want to understand
who has the problem, what they do today instead, and what would have to be true
for a new tool to actually get used. Software that solves the wrong problem
elegantly is still the wrong software.

**Scope to a first release.** We would rather ship something narrow that works
than spend a year on something broad that never launches. The first version
should be small enough to finish and useful enough to be worth finishing.

**Build in the open.** You see progress as it happens, not at a milestone
meeting six weeks later. If something is turning out harder than we thought, you
hear it when we find out.

**Ship, then learn.** Release is the middle of the process, not the end. Real
usage tells you things no amount of planning will, and the versions after launch
are usually where a product becomes good.
